Who We Are

Our GNC Committee

A group of friends from Gandhi Chowk, Wyra that has organised Ganeshotsav with devotion, music, annadanam and mass prasadam distribution every year.

Sri Lakshmi Ganapathi Utsava Committee brings together families of our locality to celebrate Ganesh Chaturthi with daily aarti, cultural programs, community meals and an eco-friendly visarjan procession. Every rupee donated goes directly toward the mandap, decorations, prasadam and community activities accounted for each year.
