Privacy Policy

GNC – Ganeshotsav 2026

Sri Lakshmi Ganapathi Utsava Committee

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the GNC – Ganeshotsav 2026 mobile application ("App") handles information when you use the App.

The App is provided for the Sri Lakshmi Ganapathi Utsava Committee and is intended to provide festival information, schedules, announcements, winners, gallery content, committee information, donor information and links to relevant festival resources.

We respect your privacy. The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information in order to use its primary features.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not directly collect or require users to submit personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, password, photographs or payment information.

The App does not provide user registration, login, commenting, messaging or user-generated-content features.

Festival information displayed in the App may include names of committee members, donors, prize winners and other individuals associated with the festival. This information is published for festival and community information purposes and is not collected from users through the App.

3. Information Retrieved from Online Services

The App displays festival information that is maintained by the Sri Lakshmi Ganapathi Utsava Committee through its online website and associated data services.

This may include:

  • Festival schedules and event timings
  • Committee information
  • Donor information
  • Prize information and winners
  • Festival gallery information
  • Frequently asked questions
  • Other festival announcements and updates

This information is used solely to display the relevant festival content to App users.

4. External Links

The App may provide links to external services such as WhatsApp and Google Maps for user convenience.

When you choose to open an external service, you may leave the App and interact directly with that third-party service. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms of service.

We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party services.

5. Location Information

The App does not continuously collect, track or store your device's location.

The App may provide a link to Google Maps so that users can voluntarily obtain directions to the festival location. Any location information used after opening Google Maps is handled by Google according to Google's own privacy practices.

6. Cookies and Tracking

The App does not intentionally use cookies, advertising trackers, behavioral tracking technologies or third-party advertising SDKs for tracking users.

The App does not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

7. Advertising

The App does not currently display advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or intentionally share personal information collected through the App with third parties.

When users voluntarily open third-party services such as WhatsApp or Google Maps, those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the operation of the App. The App does not maintain user accounts or store user-submitted personal information on our servers.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

The App does not maintain user accounts or a user database and does not intentionally store personal information submitted by users.

Because the App does not provide account creation, there is no personal user account to delete.

Festival information may be updated, corrected or removed by the Sri Lakshmi Ganapathi Utsava Committee as appropriate.

11. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. No account or personal information is required to use the App.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its features or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of the App, please contact the Sri Lakshmi Ganapathi Utsava Committee.
